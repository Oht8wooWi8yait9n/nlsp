Summary / Description

The Surface Sampler Kit (SSK) is used to collect microbial samples from the spacecraft surfaces.

The Surface Sampling Kit (SSK) is a Nomex pouch that has a pair of scissors tethered to the inside of the cover. It contains a "Sharpie" marker pen, colony density chart (used for inflight visual analysis of colony growth on the slides) and ten sampling packets.

The sampling packets contain all items necessary for obtaining bacterial and fungal samples from an exposed surface. Each packet contains two Biotest Hycon Contact Slides (one trypticase soy agar (TSA) media slide and one rose bengal agar (RB) media slide), one tube of 0.5% phosphate buffer, one tube of freezer media with an external tube, two biological specimen bags and a Biocide wipe/towelette. The slide media is covered with a transparent cover and slide seal. Each slide has a label for recording date, sampling site, and visual analysis results.
