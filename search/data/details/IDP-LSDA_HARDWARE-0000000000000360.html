Summary / Description

The subject loading device (SLD) is the restraint system used to secure an exercising subject to a treadmill in a microgravity environment. There are two SLDs required for each treadmill, one on each side of the treadmill chassis. The SLDs are rectangular enclosures measuring 18" X 9.5" X 5.5", and weighing approximately 27 pounds each. Together the two SLDs provide loading from 40 to 220 pounds. The heart of the SLD is an adjustable 17-7 precipitation-hardening steel torsion spring/hub assembly with a steel cable attached that provides tension to the harness coupled to the spring/hub assembly. A motor and controller assembly enables the restraint force to be varied under computer control. The SLD cables can be attached directly to the subject harness, or in series with bungees to achieve smoother loading.
