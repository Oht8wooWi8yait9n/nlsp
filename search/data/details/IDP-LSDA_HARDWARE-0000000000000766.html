Water Recovery System (WRS): Air Evaporation System (AES)

Record ID: 1094
Official Source: National Aeronautics and Space Administration Life Sciences Data Archive
View Official Record on NASA NLSP →

Summary / Description

The Air Evaporation Subsystem (AES) processed approximately 15% of the water not recovered by the Reverse Osmosis (RO) subsystem of the Water Recovery System (WRS). The AES used a distillation process to treat the concentrated wastewater. Dissolved salts were retained on a fiber wick housed in the evaporator section of the subsystem. Water vapor was condensed and collected in the AES condensate tank.

The AES treated the brine from the RO subsystem; it ran continuously while brine was available to be treated in the RO brine tank. The air evaporation injection system injected 250 ml of brine every 20 to 25 minutes. Injection was keyed off of weight scales that determined the mass of water recovered versus the mass that had been injected on an injection by injection basis. So, if 250 ml were injected, the next injection would not occur until 250 ml had been recovered in the condensate tank. If the RO brine tank reached its low level set point, the air evaporation system heater and blower were disabled until the RO ran again to provide more brine to be treated. The condensate from the system was collected in the AES condensate tank which is pumped to the RO product tank once the high level sensor is tripped. This pump stopped once the low level sensor was tripped or when the RO began.

The air evaporation heater maintained a 58 °C air stream at the wick inlet. The cooling water had to be less than 7 °C to effectively recover the condensate that evaporated off of the wick.

Every two to three weeks the wick drying procedure was used. During this time, no brine was injected, and the wick was dried to a weight of approximately 3 kg. This process took 30 to 60 minutes. The dry wick was then removed and replaced with a new wick. The new wick will be initialized with three injections of brine of approximately 1000 ml each.
