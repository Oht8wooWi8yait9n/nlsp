Atmospheric Analyzer

Record ID: 1084
Official Source: National Aeronautics and Space Administration Life Sciences Data Archive
View Official Record on NASA NLSP →

Summary / Description

The atmospheric analyzer consisted of a cylindrical housing that was 2.5 cm in diameter and 12.7 cm long. It contained two glass tubes filled with Tenex gas chromatograph absorbent. The air flow through each tube was regulated by a fixed orifice with a flow rate of approximately 200 cm³ per minute. The air flow rate was obtained by attaching the atmospheric analyzer to the spacecraft vacuum system. A total air sample of about 30 liters was collected.

The metallic parts of the atmospheric analyzer (body, caps, and jets) were made of aluminum. Other materials machined from Teflon and Viton were used as seals. The sample tubes were made of Pyrex glass, 50 mm x 10 mm. They contained 4.5 ml of absorbent per tube. A single layer of Teflon tape insulated the tubes from the aluminum housing. Three Viton washers were installed in each tube to serve both as seals and as elastic buffers. A groove was provided for the washer at the air inlet, in order to keep tension on the tubes. The tension was necessary to assure a tight seal was made between the tube and the jet orifice plate, and the tubes and main body.
