Summary / Description

Dataset represents the HARV RNA-seq assays in the form of graphs, spreadsheets, and report. Detailed data descriptions can be found below and included with each figure legend (or notes in spreadsheets). Treatment details follow the dataset description.

Figure 1: Distribution of gene functional categories differentially expressed during normal gravity growth relative to simulated microgravity. RNA isolated from 8-hour normal gravity and simulated microgravity S. mutans HARV cultures (n=3 independent experiments per growth condition) was subjected to RNA-seq transcriptome profiling as described in Materials and Methods. Differential expression and statistical analysis was carried out using a cutoff FDR-adjusted p value of 0.05. Of this dataset, those genes with a log2 (fold-change) value of at least ± 1.0 were grouped by functional classification based on DAVID gene functional categorization analysis, NCBI gene annotations and KEGG pathway analysis. Total number of up-regulated genes (log2 fold-change = 1.0; black bars) = 94; total number of down-regulated genes (log2 fold-change = -1.0; grey bars) = 153.

Figure 2: qRT-PCR validation of RNA-seq data. RNA isolated from microgravity and normal gravity S. mutans HARV cultures (n=3 each) was converted to cDNA and qRT-PCR was performed using the indicated primers. Amplification of gyrB served as the reference gene. In each case the normal gravity sample was used as the calibrator, and fold change was calculated using the Livak method. *Denotes significant difference relative to normal gravity (T-test, p<0.05). **Denotes difference approaching significance relative to normal gravity (T-test, p<0.08). All data represent the average ± SEM.

Figure 3: Metacyc analysis of RNA-seq data. The top three metabolic pathways to which statistically significant differentially- expressed genes matched were mixed-acid fermentation (A), arginine biosynthesis (acetyl cycle) (B), and pyrmidine deoxynucleotide de novo biosynthesis (C). Heat-map indicates log2-fold change in gene expression (normal gravity/simulated microgravity).

For each experiment, three simulated microgravity and three normal gravity HARV cultures were inoculated and grown as described for the metabolomics and end-point growth curve analysis. For each experiment, one simulated microgravity and one normal gravity HARV were each harvested at t = 8 h. After harvesting (as described for end-point growth curves and metabolomics), a 10-ml aliquot of each HARV culture was centrifuged at 3,000 RPM, 4°C for 20 minutes and immediately placed on ice. Culture supernatants were decanted and 1 ml RNAlater (Ambion) was added to each cell pellet prior to immediate storage at -80°C. RNA was isolated with the RNeasy Kit (Qiagen) and FASTPREP lysing matrix B tubes (MP Biomedical). Each RNA sample was then subjected to a second DNAseq treatment using the TURBO DNA-free™ Kit (Thermo Fisher Scientific) per the manufacturer’s protocols. Lack of contaminating genomic DNA in each RNA sample was then determined using quantitative real-time PCR (i.e. no cDNA synthesis step) and S. mutans-specific primers prior to sending = 5 µg of each RNA sample to Seqwright Genomic Services (Houston, TX), who performed all steps of the RNA-seq workflow, data curation, differential expression and corresponding statistical analysis. In brief, each RNA sample (> 2.0 purity ration) was subjected to an mRNA enrichment step and generation of a library of cDNA template molecules of known strand origin using the TruSeq Total mRNA Sample Preparation Kit (Illumina). Sequencing was performed using the Illumina HiSeq 2500 platform, generating 2 x 100bp read lengths for a total of 2 x 10M reads per sample. Illumina CASAVA software was used to create genomic builds and count reads, whereas TopHat-2.0.8b and Cufflinks-2.0.1 were used to align reads to the S. mutans UA159 genome (Genbank accession # NC_004350.2) and perform DE analysis, respectively
