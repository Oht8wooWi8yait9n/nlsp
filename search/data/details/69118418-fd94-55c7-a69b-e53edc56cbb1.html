Photosynthesis and Metabolism of Super Dwarf Wheat in Microgravity (PESTO)

Record ID: 9601269
Official Source: National Aeronautics and Space Administration Life Sciences Data Archive
View Official Record on NASA NLSP →

Summary / Description

OBJECTIVES:

The photosynthetic rate of higher plants is a critical component of plant-based atmospheric regeneration systems being proposed for long-duration space missions. It is only through direct measurement of photosynthesis in microgravity that an informed decision on the feasibility and design of these systems can be made. The specific objectives of this research are: 1) Determine the effect of microgravity on CO2 and light response curves of wheat; 2) Determine the effect of microgravity on metabolism and electron transport processes associated with photosynthetic and respiratory gas exchange; 3) Determine the effect of microgravity on carbohydrate partitioning in wheat; 4) Determine the effect of microgravity on gas exchange, including H2O, over range of atmospheric vapor pressure deficits; and 5) Utilize the knowledge gained to understand the response of plants grown under elevated CO2 conditions of commercial controlled environment crop production systems.

APPROACH:

To test the hypothesis that net carbon exchange rates are sustained in microgravity, we measured and characterized the CO2 and light response curves for a wheat photosynthetic canopy grown in microgravity. Net carbon exchange and water movement through the canopy were also be measured over a defined range of vapor pressure deficits. Postflight analysis of the tissue for primary photosynthesis parameters, including PSI, PSII, electron transport, and carbohydrate partitioning, were made and correlated to inflight data.

RESULTS:

The PESTO experiment determined there was reduction in whole chain electron transport (13%), PSII activity (13%), and PSI activity (16%) under saturating light and 4000 umol mol-1 CO2 conditions in microgravity-exposed plants. There was no difference to ground controls when measured for canopy level photosynthetic rates, CO2 compensation, and photosynthetic photon flux levels in developing plants.
