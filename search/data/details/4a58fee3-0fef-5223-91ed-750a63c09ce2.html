Shuttle Condensate Collection for the International Space Station

Record ID: RME 1331
Official Source: National Aeronautics and Space Administration Life Sciences Data Archive
View Official Record on NASA NLSP →

Summary / Description

OBJECTIVES:
The main objective of this study was to collect Shuttle humidity condensate to support 1) tests of the Russian water processor for Service Module and 2) track rate and composition during free flight versus docked phase.

APPROACH:
1. Setup: retrieve hose from floor hatch (MD21 G); connect valve to hose; connect CWC.
2. Purge: attach a sample bag; 3-way valve to Collect Sample; 1 hour purge. 3. Predock samples: attach new sample bag; check hourly; 1 liter in approximately two to three hour fill..
4. Docked phase: 3-way valve to Fill CWC; check daily; valve off at hatch closure.
5. Post-hatch closure sample: new sample bag; check hourly; approximately two to three hour fill.
6. Post-separation sample: new sample bag; check hourly; approximately two to three hour fill.
7. Stowage: Samples in fresh food tray (MF28K); CWC in Starboard Ceiling Bag.

RESULTS:
Results for this study aren’t available.
