Summary / Description

Expedition 72 began on September 23, 2024, with the undocking of the Soyuz MS-25 (71S) spacecraft from the International Space Station (ISS). The departing crew included Oleg Kononenko (RSA), Nicolai Chub (RSA), and Tracy Caldwell-Dyson (NASA).

The remaining crew included Commander Sunita Williams (NASA) and Flight Engineers Michael Barratt (NASA), Matthew Dominick (NASA), Jeanette Epps (NASA), Alexander Grebenkin (RSA), Butch Wilmore (NASA), Aleksey Ovchinin (RSA), Ivan Vagner (RSA), and Donald Pettit (NASA). These remaining crew members transitioned into Expedition 72.

On September 29, 2025, the crew grew by two upon the docking of SpaceX Crew-9 carrying Flight Engineers Nick Hague (Crew-9 Commander, NASA) and Aleksandr Gorbunov (Crew-9 Pilot, RSA).

The unusually large transitioning crew resulted from changes in Expedition 71/Expedition 72 transition crew rotation plans caused by weather concerns and technical concerns. The return to Earth of the SpaceX Crew-8 crew (Barratt, Dominick, Epps, and Grebenkin) faced delays due to persistent weather concerns in probable landing zones. The weather finally cleared enough for the crew to board the Crew-8 spacecraft, undock on October 23, and return home to a splash down near Pensacola, Florida on October 25. Technical concerns led to the Boeing CST-100 Starliner spacecraft returning home uncrewed during Expedition 71. The Boe-CFT crew (Williams and Wilmore) joined the Crew-9 crew for the return trip home.

Expedition 72 crewmembers continued to operate and maintain the station's systems as well as performed the many science experiments in progress, initiated new investigations, and undertook technology demonstrations.

Three spacewalks occurred during the expedition: Russian crewmembers Ovchinin and Wagner completed EVA R-63 on December 19, 2024; US crewmembers Hague and Williams performed a 6 hour EVA focused on maintenance and upgrades to the stationon January 16, 2025; US crewmembers Williams and Willmore performed a 5 hour 26 minute EVA to remove a radio frequency group antenna assembly on January 30, 2025.

Preparation for Expedition 73 began on March 14th, 2025 with the launch of SpaceX Crew-10, the 10th operational flight of NASA’s Commercial Crew Program. Crew members included Anne McClain (Crew-10 Commander, NASA), Nichole Ayers (Crew-10 Pilot, NASA), and Crew-10 Mission Specialists Takuya Onishi (JAXA), and Kirill Peskov (RSA).

Space-X Crew-9 undocked on March 18, 2025 and splashed down near Florida bringing Hague, Gorbunov, Williams, and Wilmore home. On April 8, 2025, the Soyuz MS-27 (73S) launched from the Baikonur Cosmodrome and docked to the station with Sergey Ryzhikov (RSA), Alexey Zubritsky (RSA), and Jonny Kim (NASA) aboard.

Finally, the Soyuz MS-26 (72S) undocked and returned to Earth on April 19th, 2025, carrying Ovchinin, Vagner, and Pettit home.

The undocking of the Soyuz MS-26 marked the end of Expedition 72 on April 19th, 2025 at approximately 21:57 UTC.
