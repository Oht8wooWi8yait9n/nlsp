Pharmacotherapeutics of Intranasal Scopolamine

Record ID: NCC958NA00404
Official Source: National Aeronautics and Space Administration Life Sciences Data Archive
View Official Record on NASA NLSP →

Summary / Description

OBJECTIVES:
This study was designed to validate an Investigational New Drug (IND) for the treatment of space motion sickness. The aims of this experiment were to (a) establish the pharmacodynamics of IN SCOP with four escalating dose levels between 0.1 and 0.4 mg, (b) examine the efficacy of two dose levels of IN SCOP for prevention and treatment of motion sickness, and (c) determine if bioavailability and pharmacodynamics of SCOP are altered in a simulated microgravity environment.

APPROACH:
This funded research has resulted in a new partnership with the Naval Aerospace Medical Research Association (NAMRL). NAMRL has conducted a clinical trail to compare the efficacy of four drugs, including this IND drug, in a parallel study design at suppressing motion sickness. Due to lack of perceived difference between the four different treatments the study was modified in an extension to test the comparative efficacy of the current test article, INSCOP and oral scopolamine, in a crossover study design in 16 subjects. Clinical aspects of this study were completed in early 2008.

RESULTS:
This is a National Space Biomedical Research Institute (NSBRI) ground-based investigation and NASA does not currently have an agreement to archive this data in the LSDA.
