Summary / Description

OBJECTIVES:
The Spaceflight Standard Measures project will ensure that an optimized, minimal set of measures is consistently captured from all International Space Station (ISS) crewmembers until the end of ISS in order to characterize the human in space. The data collected will support the following objectives:
  1. Enable high level monitoring of countermeasure effectiveness
  2. Enable meaningful interpretation of health and performance outcomes
  3. Inform and support future hypothesis-driven planetary-mission-enabling research
APPROACH:
The opportunity to participate in the Spaceflight Standard Measures project will be offered to all United States Operating Segment (USOS) crewmembers. A defined set of core measurements, representative of many of the human spaceflight risks, will be performed. Data collected will include analysis of blood, urine, and saliva, questionnaires, and core measurements from the physiological and psychological domains deemed critical to human spaceflight. Data collections will occur preflight, inflight, and postflight.
