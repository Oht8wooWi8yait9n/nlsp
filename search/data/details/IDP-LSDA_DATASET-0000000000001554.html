Summary / Description

Dataset represents the HARV competence assays in the form of two graphs. Detailed data descriptions can be found below and included with each figure legend. Treatment details follows the dataset description.

Figure 1: S. mutans competence phenotype assessed in simulated microgravity and normal gravity HARV cultures. Simulated microgravity and normal gravity HARV cultures, as well as a parallel static Falcon tube control culture, were each grown for 1.5 hours in biofilm media prior to addition of 2 µg pOri23 plasmid DNA. All three cultures were then grown or 2.5 more hours followed by harvest and serial dilution plating on BHI agar ± 10 µg/ml erythromycin (selective antibiotic to monitor plasmid transformation). Transformation efficiency was calculated as the percentage of transformants (CFU/ml on BHI + erythromycin) among total viable cells (CFU/ml on BHI). Data represent the average of n=3 independent experiments. Error bars =SEM.

Figure 2: Quantitative competence assay. S. mutans HARV cultures were grown for 1.5 hours in BHI containing 5% horse serum prior to addition of 2 µg plasmid DNA. HARV cultures were then grown for 2.5 more hours followed by harvest and serial dilution plating on BHI agar ± selective antibiotic. Transformation efficiency was calculated as calculated as the percentage of transformants (CFU/ml on BHI + selective antibiotic) among total viable cells (CFU/ml on BHI). Data represents the average of n=3 independent experiments. Error bars = SEM.

Treatment simulated normal gravity and simulated microgravity HARV (10 ml size) growth, 37ºC, 5% CO2, 30 RPM in Brain Heart Infusion (BHI) broth or Biofilm media containing glucose and sucrose (as indicated in graph figure legends). HARVS were removed from rotator bases after 90 minutes growth, and 2 µg of unmethylated plasmid DNA was added to each reactor. Microgravity and normal gravity HARVs were then incubated for 2.5 h prior to serial dilution and plating of each culture on BHI agar ±10 µg/ml erythromycin (Erm; selectable antibiotic for plasmid). CFU/ml were determined after 48 h plate growth, and transformation efficiencies were calculated as the percentage of transformants (BHI-Erm CFU/ml) among the total viable cells (BHI CFU/ml). For each biofilm media experiment, a non-rotating control culture (15- ml Falcon tube containing 15 ml of S. mutans culture) was grown in parallel and assessed for competence as described above.
