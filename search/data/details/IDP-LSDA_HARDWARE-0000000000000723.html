Summary / Description

The Physiological Signal Conditioner (PSC) single housing assembly is a compact, configurable, lightweight, battery powered instrument designed to monitor an array of physiological signals from crewmembers. The PSC can be configured to monitor an array of array of physiological signals, including electrocardiograph (ECG), electroencephalograph (EEG), electromyograph (EMG), electrooculograph (EOG) and skin potential response (SPR). The PSC assembly is designed to pick up physiological signals from surface electrodes and to amplify and filter those signals. The analog output of the PSC is then received by other instruments for recording or further processing.

The PSC single housing assembly is powered by two (2) 7.5 V battery pack assemblies that will allow the PSC assembly to operate for 100 hours. Each battery pack assembly contains five (5) 1.55V silver oxide VARTA V350 button cell batteries in series. A battery access door on the PSC assembly allows in-flight battery replacement without the use of tools. A flashing green LED provides a visual indication that the battery is operating either at acceptable power levels or falls below 6.5 volts. The LED flashing rate of once every 5 seconds for normal operating power levels increases to approximately 10 times the normal rate when the battery voltage drops below 6.5 volts. Battery power is applied and removed by connecting and disconnecting the PSC ECG data cable to the PSC output connector. This is accomplished by a jumper in the PSC output cable assembly between the "V+ battery out" pin and a "V+ battery in" pin, and another jumper between a "V- battery out" pin and "V- battery in" pin at the PSC's output connector.

All electrical leads, which are to be connected to the subject, are isolated with two FET current limiter stages in series with each other. Each current limiter stage independently limits the current that passes to or from the PSC to less than 10 uA for all DC voltages up to 200 V and less than 15 uA for all AC voltages up to 200 V (0 to 1,000 Hz) and independently have a breakdown voltage greater than 300 V. The total impedance (insertion resistance) of the current limiters at any electrode input does not exceed 200 Kohms (DC to 1,000 Hz) for currents less than 10 uA.

The PSC is flown as a kit containing the following components: a Nomex pouch containing 2 PSCs, a PSC ECG Data cable (sends ECG data to the Pressure-Flow Module(PFM), the ECG electrode leads, a spare battery pack (separate Nomex pouch containing 4 PSC battery packs), a GASMAP filter pack (separate Nomex pouch containing 12 GASMAP filters), and a GASMAP catheter pack (separate Nomex pouch containing 24 GASMAP catheters). The spare battery pack, GASMAP filter pack, and GASMAP catheter pack are housed in separate Nomex pouches so that they can be easily replenished on future flights.
