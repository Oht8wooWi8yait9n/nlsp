Mechanisms and Functional Consequences of Protein Kinase C Isoform Translocation Inhibition in Monocytes Exposed to Microgravity (PKinase)

Record ID: ARC00XX60
Official Source: National Aeronautics and Space Administration Life Sciences Data Archive
View Official Record on NASA NLSP →

Summary / Description

OBJECTIVES:

This study investigates the mechanisms by which microgravity affects protein kinase C (PKC) signalling in monocytes and the functional consequences of these changes on downstream signalling events, gene expression and cell fate. This is important since many earthbound biological processes depend on this pathway and indeed the immune system itself is dependent on normal PKC signalling. We are analyzing the expression of patterns of gene expression induced by differentiation inducing agonists in the U937 human monocyte cell line in microgravity and 1.g, and are determining the functional effects of alterations in PKC signalling that occur in microgravity as well as on earth. Since all of Earths life forms have developed in a 1-g environment we will also learn if 1-g is necessary for full monocyte function.

APPRAOCH:

The experimental approach consists of stimulating PKC isoform activation in U937 cells using differentiation inducing agonists in microgravity and a 1.g on-board centrifuge. Gene chips will be used to identify the changes in the pattern of gene expression between microgravity, to identify which PKC isoform signalling pathways are affected by microgravity. Quantative reverse transcriptase – polymerase chain reaction (qRT-PCR) will be used to confirm expression of key genes identified from the gene array data. The sub-cellular localization of PKC isoforms and expression of key surface activation markers will be determined in formaldehyde fixed samples. Collectively, these data will allow us to explore in detail the consequences of perturbed PKC signalling in microgravity at the level of gene expression and cell fate.

We will analyze the expression of patterns of gene expression induced by differentiation inducing agonists in the U937 human monocyte cell line in microgravity and 1.g, to determine the functional effects of alterations in PKC signalling that occur in microgravity.

RESULTS:

Flight RNA was excellent; we have completed our analysis of gene expression. Analysis of adhesion and adhesion molecules of the formaldehyde fixed samples will wait until we know gene abundance.

No data submitted. Summarized and analyzed data may be available through publications.
