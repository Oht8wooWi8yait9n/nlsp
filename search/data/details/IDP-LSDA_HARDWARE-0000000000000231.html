Summary / Description

Formaldehyde badges, which rely on passive diffusion, are purchased from Air Quality Research (Durham, North Carolina), which markets them to the industrial hygiene community. When received at JSC, the frame of each badge is modified to be a 2 1/2-inch square and marked for NASA applications. Each lot is tested for uptake of formaldehyde and background response. Once exposed to air, the dry collector converts formaldehyde to a stable product. The diffusion rate is controlled by a permeable membrane between the collector and the sample atmosphere. The badges are passive collection devices.
