Identification of Functional Metabolomic Alterations during the Simulated Spaceflight Environment

Record ID: NNX16AG03G
Official Source: National Aeronautics and Space Administration Life Sciences Data Archive
View Official Record on NASA NLSP →

Summary / Description

OBJECTIVES:
The objectives of this experiment is to identify novel early biomarkers in plasma and urine to detect and monitor the progression of a number of physiological disturbances due to prolonged microgravity and CO2 exposure as experienced by crewmembers on long duration missions. These physiological manifestations include:
  1. Visual Impairment/Intracranial Pressure (VIIP);
  2. Sub-clinical or environmentally induced cardiovascular disease;
  3. Muscle atrophy and decreased muscle strength; and
  4. Bone loss

APPROACH:
Targeted and untargeted metabolomics will be applied to plasma and urine collected longitudinally rom study participants undergoing a 30 day six-degree head down bed rest combined with ambient 0.5% CO2. Metabolomics will be followed by a novel cell-based metabolic mammalian organ system assay to address how these metabolites affect physiological processes at the cellular and organ level.

RESULTS:
Results for this study are currently not available.
