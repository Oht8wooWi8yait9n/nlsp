Summary / Description

The Human Exploration Research Analog (HERA) serves as an analog for the NASA Human Research Program (HRP) to study and evaluate impacts due to isolation, remoteness, and confined habitation. HERA is an ideal analog for research involving behavioral health and performance assessments, communication and autonomy studies, human factors evaluations, and medical capabilities assessments. Each HERA Campaign includes multiple missions simulating one or more space exploration scenarios and supporting multiple investigations. HERA is located at the Johnson Space Center in Houston, Texas.

HERA Campaign 5 consisted of 15 investigations over four 45-day missions. All four missions were completed. It should be noted that postflight data collection for the fourth and final mission was compressed to accommodate possible local actions due to national coronavirus concerns but all data collection sessions were completed.

The ingress/egress schedule for the completed Campaign was: 

  • Mission 1 (C5M1) – Complete:  Ingress: February 15, 2019; Egress: April 1, 2019
  • Mission 2 (C5M2) – Complete:  Ingress: May 24, 2019; Egress: July 8, 2019
  • Mission 3 (C5M3) – Complete:  Ingress: August 16, 2019; Egress: September 30, 2019
  • Mission 4 (C5M4) – Complete:  Ingress: January 24, 2020; Egress: March 9, 2020
