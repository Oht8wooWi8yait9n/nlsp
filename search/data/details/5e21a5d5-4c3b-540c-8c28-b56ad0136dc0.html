Summary / Description

OBJECTIVE:
The definition of dark focus is the resting state of visual accommodation is that refractive state to which the eye tends to return in the absence of any visual stimulus, as in complete darkness. Seven healthy in-house subjects (4 males; 3 females), ranging in age from 27 - 55 years old, participated in this study.

Neurobiological research indicates that changes in visual accommodation are associated with stresses that may occur during space mission. Measures of accommodation may be used to index onset and extent of space motion sickness and other stress of spacelfith. An accurate measure of accommodation may be used to signal that visual performances may be degraded.

APPROACH:
To participate in the study, the subjects were not asked to alter their normal daily routine. Prior to each test session, the subjects completed a short questionnaire that documented their recent pre-test activities. All subjects reported that they were in their usual state of fitness, and that they had a sufficient amount of sleep within the last 24 hours. Although caffeine consumption was not specifically controlled, subjects reported having three of fewer cups of coffee throughout an entire 24 hour period on test days. Six of the seven subjects were nonsmokers, and the 1 subject who was a smoker routinely smoked 2-3 cigars each morning. On average, each subject had sat in front of a video display terminal 1-2 hours prior to each test session. Only 1 subject required glasses for corrected vision, and did wear corrective lenses when performing the test.

Measurements of dark focus were made twice daily, in the morning and afternoon, on four consecutive days. The test sessions were scheduled for approximately the same time of day for each subject across the four days. During each test session, three measurements of dark focus were made in a dark room. The subject was instructed to look in to he Dark-focus Measuring Device (DFMC) using his/her dominant eye while keeping the other eye closed. Sliding the focus adjustment rod back and forth, the subject adjusted the focus of the fiberoptic light until a sharply focused light image appeared. The subjects was instructed to close his/her eyes while the operator recorded the measurement of the dark focus then offset the focus of the fiberoptic light by sliding the focus adjustment rod prior to repeating the test procedure two times for a total of 3 measurements.

RESULTS:
Results are not available for this study.
