Microbial Water Monitoring using a Multiplexed Lateral Flow Assay

Record ID: Water_Monitoring
Official Source: National Aeronautics and Space Administration Life Sciences Data Archive
View Official Record on NASA NLSP →

Summary / Description

OBJECTIVES:
Multi-agent immunoassays for monitoring the integrity of the food and water will be essential for maintaining astronaut health during long-duration space flight. Similar technology may also be applied for monitoring the dynamics of the ecology within bioreactors or for clinical testing of astronauts during flight. Ideally, the design of these devices should be such that a minimum of crew interaction is required for their maintenance or use. The investigator’s laboratory is developing two types of multiplexed immunoassays for water monitoring that incorporate quantum dots for measuring the concentration of several types of pathogen in a single test. Both immunoassay tests are intended to be used in conjunction with the water test kit (WTK) currently used on the International Space Station (ISS). Lateral flow assays (LFA) and flow-through assays (FTA) are being developed that provides single-use testing for the presence of analytes within a fluid sample. LFA’s are strip tests that are typically read visually and require no active fluidics system since they rely only on capillary action for fluid flow. FTA may be constructed on microbial filters to allow in-place testing of microbes within the current WTK system used on the ISS.
