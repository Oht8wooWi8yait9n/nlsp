Electron Microscopic Analysis of Archived Tissues

Record ID: ARC00704
Official Source: National Aeronautics and Space Administration Life Sciences Data Archive
View Official Record on NASA NLSP →

Summary / Description

Objectives:

To determine whether selected archived tissues stored in primary fixative are adequately preserved to warrant further processing and morphological analysis of spaceflight-induced changes at the electron microscopic level.

Approach:

Small samples (1-8 mm3) of the primary specimens will be excised, rinsed, postfixed in osmium tetraoxide, dehydrated and embedded in plastic resin for ultramicrotomy. Because the organs were fixed by immersion, a gradient of preservation from good to poor is expected for the superficial to deep portions. To determine the quality of preservation rigorously, for each specimen a superficial region will be compared with a deep region. Thin sections (70 nm) will be cut, picked up on grids, poststained and examined by transmission electron microscopy.

A set of specimens for each time point consist of 10 organs sampled superficial and deep from 5 rats. Kidney and testis are good organs to study because the organs are sensitive to poor fixation.
Representative light and electron micrographs will be taken and evaluated for morphological integrity. A summary report, including appraisal of the quality of the specimen for further scientific study, will be prepared for each specimen.
