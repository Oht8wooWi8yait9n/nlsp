Summary / Description

The Audio Subsystem, a component of the Visual and Vestibular Investigation System (VVIS), allows communication between the subject and the Body Rotation Device (BRD) operator, provides for recording of the subject's voice perception reports during the experiment for later analysis, and supplies a masking noise to the subject. This subsystem includes a subject headset, audio tape player, and Wireless Crew Communication System (WCCS) Crew Remote Unit (CRU).

Subject Headset

The subject headset has a microphone that is integrated into the BRD which allows the subject's voice comments to be recorded on the experiment video tapes. The headset also allows the subject and operator to communicate with each other and supplies the masking noise (music background tape) to the subject.

Audio Tape Player

The Audio Tape Player supplies a masking noise to help isolate the subject from any Spacelab noise that could indicate a point of reference. Music tapes are provided to help maintain subject alertness. The Audio Player is also linked to the standard Spacelab WCCS to allow communication with the operator. The operator's voice overrides the music background when present. Two tape players (primary and backup) are available. The tape player uses one AA battery for operation. The VSK contains a AA Battery Kit with 28 batteries.

Wireless Crew Communication System (WCCS) Crew Remote Unit (CRU)

The WCCS CRU enables the BRD subject and operator to communicate to each other on special radio frequency. Four standard CRUs were modified with an additional channel. A special channel, marked with a green dot on each modified CRU, is used for the Vestibular experiment operations. The CRU is mounted on the BRD and was termed the "Subject CRU". Another CRU, termed the "Operator CRU", was brought into the Spacelab for the BRD operator. Two other CRUs were backup units. When set to the green dot channel, operator-subject communication was enabled. Should contact with the ground or other crewmembers be desired, the operator switched to another channel for those communications.
