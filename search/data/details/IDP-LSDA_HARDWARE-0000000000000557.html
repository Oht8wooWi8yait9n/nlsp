Biological Sample Holding System (BSHS)

Record ID: 599
Official Source: National Aeronautics and Space Administration Life Sciences Data Archive
View Official Record on NASA NLSP →

Summary / Description

The Biological Sample Holding System (BSHS) is composed of two main subassemblies, the Sampleholder "Cards" and "Cages". Sample vials (blood, urine, or saliva) are put into the Sampleholder Cards which are inserted into Sampleholder Cages into either the Spacelab Thermoelectric Freezer (STEF) or Thermoelectric Freezing Module (TE-FM) for quick cooling. They are then placed in the Thermoelectric Holding Module (TE-HM) if operating on the Middeck. If operating in the Middeck on a Spacelab mission, the Sampleholder Cages are transferred from the Middeck TE-HM to the Spacelab Refrigerator/Freezer when the Middeck TE-HM becomes full.

There are several types of Cages that make up the entire BSHS. The Cage assembly dimensions are:

  1. Blood Sampleholder Cage Assembly:

    2. Stowed Dimensions: 36.5 x 24.8 x 2.0 cm (14.4 x 9.8 x 0.80 inches)
    Open Dimensions: 36.5 x 11.6 x 13.8 cm (14.4 x 4.6 x 5.4 inches)
    Mass: 0.70 kg (1.54 lbs)

  2. Urine/Saliva Sampleholder Cage Assembly Long:
    Stowed Dimensions: 28.9 x 33.8 x 2.5 cm (11.38 x 13.40 x 0.80 inches)
    Open Dimensions: 28.9 x 11.6 x 23.2 cm (11.38 x 4.56 x 9.13 inches)
    Mass: 0.78 kg (1.72 lbs)

  3. Urine/Saliva Sampleholder Cage Assembly Short:
    Stowed Dimensions: 27.3 x 19.4 x 2.0 cm (10.8 x13.40 x 0.80 inches)
    Open Dimensions: 27.3 x 11.6 x 8.9 cm (10.8 x 4.6 x 3.5 inches)
    Mass: 0.41 kg (0.89 lbs)

  4. Urine/Saliva Sampleholder Cage Assembly Center:
    Stowed Dimensions: 31.8 x 22.3 x 2.0 cm (12.5 x 8.8 x 0.8 inches)
    Open Dimensions: 31.8 x 11.6 x 11.1 cm (12.5 x 4.6 x 4.4 inches)
    Mass: 0.75 kg (1.66 lbs)

Each cage is filled with multiple cards. A card is a thin metal frame utilized to hold multiple sample tubes. The cards are then designed to slip into the slots in the cage. The slots are equipped with a spring loaded locking device that prevents the cards from slipping out.

Card style 1, which is currently utilized for blood samples, accepts 5 or 7 ml tubes. Tubes can be plastic or glass (glass tubes must have Teflon shrink wrap on exterior). Tubes are placed on the cards' alternating tops and bottoms due to a close fit. Cards designed for "blood tubes" hold 7 tubes and are anodized red. The dimensions of the card are 11.25 x 1.78 x 10.80 cm (4.43 x 7.70 x 4.25 inches) and its mass is 0.03 kg (0.06 lbs).

Card style 3, which is currently utilized for urine samples, accepts 20 ml tubes from the Urine Monitoring System (UMS) hardware. Tubes are place on the cards alternating tops and bottoms due to a close fit. Cards designed for "Urine" hold 4 tubes and are anodized. The urine sampleholder card dimensions are 11.25 x 2.13 x x12.07 cm (4.43 x 0.84 x 4.75 inches) and its mass is 0.03 kg (0.07 lbs).

The number of samples that the BSHS can hold are as follows:

Blood Cage: 6 slide guides x 3 cards per slide x 7 samples per card = 126 total blood samples.
Long Urine/Saliva Cage: 9 slide guides x 2 cards per guide x 4 samples = 72 total samples per long urine/saliva cage.
Short Urine/Saliva Cage: 3 slide guides x 2 cards per guide x 4 samples = 24 total samples per short urine/saliva cage.
Center Urine/Saliva Cage: 4 slide guides x 2 cards per guide x 4 samples = 32 total samples per center urine/saliva cage.
