Nuclear Track Detector Package (NTDP)

Record ID: 947
Official Source: National Aeronautics and Space Administration Life Sciences Data Archive
View Official Record on NASA NLSP →

Summary / Description

Nuclear track detector packages (NTDPs) consist of several combinations of thermoluminescent dosimeters (TLD) and cellulose nitrate/CR-39/polycarbonate plastic detectors. NTDPs will allow mission integrated measurements of absorbed dose, neutron dose, and heavy ion fluences. Spectral composition with respect to charge, energy, and linear energy transfer (LET) are also measured. In addition, the NTDPs will show target fragmentations for different absorber materials and provide measurements of angular distributions of heavy ions and target fragments from different orientations throughout the International Space Station.

For target fragment studies, a selected absorber material is located between a single type of nuclear track detector. For angular measurements of the fragments, small detector stacks are located inside the packages with their surfaces viewing in different directions. Angular measurements of the heavy ions require exposure areas of the whole detector stacks in different orientations.

A typical dosimeter construction uses a stable of nuclear track detector layers. The top and bottom of such a stack is formed by monolayers of TLD layers welded in Polyethylene foils. Each NTDP also contains three strips of plastic film on which high-energy particles passing through this film will leave a track. One strip will be used for each of three perpendicular axes.
