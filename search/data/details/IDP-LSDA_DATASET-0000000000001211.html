Summary / Description

The datasets represent ATR-4 devices installed in the 2 flight AEMs. ATR-4s were turned on and installed in the flight AEMs in approximately the L-24h-to-L-20h period (ATR-4 activation at approximately 6/19/93 1110h EDT), at KSC/CCAFS/Hangar L. Animals were installed in the AEMs at approximately 6/19/93 1300h. The 2 flight AEMs were installed in the Orbiter middeck at approximately 1700h EDT 6/19/93, at approximately L-16h. The scheduled 6/20/93 morning launch was scrubbed due to poor weather. The 2 flight AEMs remained on the Orbiter middeck, and the 6/21/93 0907h EDT launch was successful. The Orbiter landed at KSC on 7/1/93 0852h EDT. The 2 flight AEMs were offloaded from the Orbiter and returned to Hangar L at approximately R+3hours (approximately 7/1/93 1200h), at which time animals were removed for processing, and ATR-4s were removed.

Within each dataset are temperature measurement timepoints separated by 225 seconds (3.75 minutes). At each temperature measurement timepoint, there are 4 temperatures (in degrees C.), representing the internal temperature sensor in the ATR-4 (channel 1), and the 3 external probes attached to each ATR-4. In each data timepoint entry in the dataset, the data read (from left to right): time; channel 1, channel 2, channel 3, channel 4. For PSE.03, the ATR-4s were configured to record 4 channels of temperature data, with channel 1 recording from the ATR-4 internal temperature sensor, and the remaining 3 channels recording data from remotely-placed external temperature probes.
