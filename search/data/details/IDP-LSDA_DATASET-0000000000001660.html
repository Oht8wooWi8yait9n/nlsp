Summary / Description

These datasets represent non-attributable data from the final report:
  • Theoretical Relation of Dose Rate to Depth: This figure represents the theoretical relation of dose rate to depth within a blood sample irradiated from one side only. Points are experimental values, the average of three repetitions of this experiment, normalized to 1.0 at t=0.0 mm.
  • Calculated axial depth-dose curve: This figure represents the calculated axial depth-dose curve for a 3-mm slab of unit density. The horizontal broken line indicates the average dose over sample thickness.
  • Irradiated Cell with Single Break Chromosome Deletion: This figure shows an irradiated cell with single break chromosome deletion. This cell contained one chromosome deletion and the resulting acentric fragments. The irradiated cells produced the expected kinds of aberrations.
  • Irradiated Cell with Two-Break Chromosome Deletion: This figure shows an irradiated cell with a typical two-break aberration, a dicentric chromosome with the resulting acentric fragments. The irradiated cells produced the expected kinds of aberrations.
  • Chromosome Preparation of a Normal Unirradiated Cell: This figure shows a chromosome preparation of a normal unirradiated cell without chromosome breaks, fragments, or rearrangements.
  • Chromosome Aberration Analyses for Experiment S004:This table shows results of chromosome aberration analyses. Frequencies of chromatid aberrations and of cells with chromosome numbers other than 2n=46 (excluding those with aberrations) are not different from those seen in other ground-based radiation experiments of the time.
  • Coefficients of Aberration Production and Fits of Deletion Data to the Model This table shows coefficients of aberration production. The data for chromosome deletion for both parts of the experiment (human and Neurospora crassa show noticeable departure from the linear dependence on dose assumed in calculating the coefficients of deletion production as shown in this table. The table also shows fits of deletion data to a quadratic model .

    Source: In Bender MA, Gooch CP, Kondo S. The Gemini-3 S-4 Spaceflight-Radiation Interaction Experiment. Radiat Res. 1967 May;31(1):91-111.
