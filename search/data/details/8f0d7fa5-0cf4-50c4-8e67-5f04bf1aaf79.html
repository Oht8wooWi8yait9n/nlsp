Perceptual Stability Across the Visual Field

Record ID: Visual_Stability
Official Source: National Aeronautics and Space Administration Life Sciences Data Archive

Summary / Description

OBJECTIVES:
Visual perception is often thought to arise directly from input to the retina. Recent experiments in neurophysiology have revealed that this simple scheme is inaccurate. Instead, the brain constructs an internal representation of visual information that is updated with each eye movement. The integration of motor signals with visual information allows us to perceive the world as stationary despite constant eye movements. The proposed experiments will investigate the neural mechanisms underlying perceptual stability and the degree to which this stability is preserved across the visual field. The results will be significant for understanding normal perception and the ways in which it may be altered in microgravity environments.
