Effect of Spaceflight on Lung Gene and Protein Expression Profiles

Record ID: ARC00XX94
Official Source: National Aeronautics and Space Administration Life Sciences Data Archive
View Official Record on NASA NLSP →

Summary / Description

OBJECTIVES:
Analyses will be completed to identify the set of lung genes that differed in expression between C57Bl/6 mice flown on Space Shuttle Atlantis, STS-135 and BALB/c mice from Space Shuttle Discovery, STS-133 missions. In a second analysis, C57Bl/6 and BALB/c mice pulmonary gene expression levels will be compared with ground control groups. Further study of these genes may yield specific candidates for subsequent clinical investigation for factors influencing pulmonary response to the space flight condition.

APPROACH:
Strain comparison of gene expression data: Analyses will be completed to identify the set of lung genes that differed in expression between C57Bl/6 mice flown on STS-135 and BALB/c mice from STS-133 missions. In a second analysis, C57Bl/6 and BALB/c mice pulmonary gene expression levels will be compared with ground control groups.

Altered expression of proteins in lung: We will apply proteomics approach using high-resolution two-dimensional gel electrophoresis (2DE) and matrix-assisted laser desorption ionization-time of flight (MALDI-TOF) mass spectrometry (MS) to evaluate relative changes in protein abundance in response to spaceflight.

Histopathological changes in lung: Characterization of the lung disease in C57Bl/6 and BALB/C mice from the flight and ground control groups will be assessed by histological analysis. Lung histological modifications will be correlated with the transcriptional up-regulation of pro-inflammatory mediators by microarray analysis.

RESULTS:
Further data analysis is in progress to characterize oxidative gene expression in RSV-infected mice that flew in space as well as in ground-based mouse models of space flight: We will test the hypothesis that hindlimb unloading (HLU) model alters the expression of antioxidant (AOEs) and H2S enzymes levels in a BALB/c mouse model of viral infection, and the results will be compared with space flight data from STS-133 mission.
