Summary / Description

This figure shows the saliva protein concentrations, secretory IgA and lysozyme levels, and saliva flow rates of the prime and backup crewmembers of Skylab 2. One graph depicts the prime crew over the preflight and postflight timeframes, and the other graph depicts the backup crew over the preflight timeframe. The X axis is divided into three sections of milliliters/minute of saliva flow, mean values for IgA and lysozyme, and mean milligrams out of 100 of total saliva protein. The IgA and lysozyme data are divided into lysozyme, lysozyme to protein ratio, IgA and IgA to protein ratio.

Frome WJ, Brown LR, Wheatcroft MG. Oral microbiology, oral immunology, and oral clinical evaluation of Skylab 1/2 crew. In: Ross C E, coll.Skylab 1/2 Preliminary Biomedical Report. Houston, Texas: National Aeronautics and Space Administration; 1973 Sep:130-149. Report No.: JSC-08439.
