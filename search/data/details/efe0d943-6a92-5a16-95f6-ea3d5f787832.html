Electronic Medical Records for Exploration Medical System

Record ID: Electronic_Med_Rec
Official Source: National Aeronautics and Space Administration Life Sciences Data Archive
View Official Record on NASA NLSP →

Summary / Description

OBJECTIVES:
The Electronic Medical Record (EMR) is an electronic system that provides a repository for medical data, images, videos, and reports and makes the information accessible to the crew in flight via a graphical user interface to assist in the diagnosis and treatment of acute and chronic medical conditions.

The system is used to access medical data for all medical encounters, especially during periods of exploration missions when contact with ground resources (e.g., flight surgeon) will either be minimal or absent. During these periods of autonomous medical care, the crew will need such a system for urgent and non-urgent medical events.

The objectives of the EMR were to:

  1. Reduce or eliminate the time required of an on-orbit crew (i.e., clinician and non-clinician) to store, access, and process medical data, images, and reports.
  2. Reduce the time required of the ground crew to access medical data, images, or reports.
  3. Ensure Crew Medical Office (CMO) access to all relevant crewmember medical data, images, or reports for improved quality of care.
  4. Ensure crew access to personal medical data, images, or reports during periods of restricted ground communication.
  5. Ensure crew ability to capture onboard medical data regardless of the state of the communication link to the ground.
  6. Ensure privacy protection of onboard crewmember medical data.

APPROACH:
Development of the OpenEMR application programming interfaces (APIs), required for integration into the Exploration Medical System, can be made available to the OpenEMR open source community for potential inclusion into future releases of the EMR software. These APIs enhance the ability to import and export data into/out of the EMR database.

RESULTS:
All required development of the EMR for integration into the greater Exploration Medical System Demonstration (EMSD) project has been completed. This includes the development of APIs, data forms, and associated intermediate software "adapters" and databases.
