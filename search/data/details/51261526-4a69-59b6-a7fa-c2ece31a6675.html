Artificial Gravity Sleep Study

Record ID: AGSLEEP
Official Source: National Aeronautics and Space Administration Life Sciences Data Archive
View Official Record on NASA NLSP →

Summary / Description

OBJECTIVES:
This measurement was conducted as part of the Artificial Gravity Pilot Study at the University of Texas Medical Branch (UTMB) in Galveston, Texas. This measurment aimed provide an objective measure of pre, in, and post bed rest sleep assessment by using periodic sleep questionnaires to assess sleep quality. Part of the Sleep Assessment protocol was a Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index (PSQI) questionnaire which was administered once before bed rest and once after bed rest. The questionnaire consists of a series of questions related to the subject's usual sleep habits during the past month. The answers provided by the subject, indicate the most accurate reply for the majority of the days and nights in the past month.

APPROACH:
A sleep diary was also be collected three times pre bed rest, nine times in-test, and six times post bed rest. The sleep diary consists of six questions relating to the subject quality of sleep. The subject answered the questions relating to their quality of sleep by indicating a 1 for very good, 2 for good, a 3 for fairly bad, and a 4 for very bad.

RESULTS:
This experiment has concluded in ground-based studies sponsored by the National Aeronautics and Space Administration's Human Adaptation and Countermeasures Division. This experiment was conducted during the Artificial Gravity Bed Rest Study.
