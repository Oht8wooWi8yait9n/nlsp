Summary / Description

The Human research Facility (HRF) Workstation is a rack-mounted, multi-purpose, high-end computer designed to support human psychological, physiological, cognitive and human factors studies. The HRF Workstation is also a general purpose experiment computer. The HRF workstation components consist of:

  • Four (4) Panel Unit (PU) Drawers
  • Small Computer System Interface (SCSI) Linear Insertion Mechanical Extraction (LIME)
  • Five (5) removable SCSI devices on board. The SCSI hard drives will contain up to 18 gigabytes of data storage.
  • SCSI Personal Computer Memory Card International Association (PCMCIA) hard drives that contain a minimum of 340 megabytes each of storage capacity.
  • A keyboard with an integrated trackball
  • A 17" high resolution monitor that will be capable of switching between Ultrasound and the Workstation with the flip of a single switch. The monitor and keyboard shall be able to connect to Bogen arms that attach to seat tracks for flexibility.

    Data Collection Inputs include: PCMCIA hard drives, SCSI hard drives, RS 232/422, Ethernet, video input/output, floppy disk, parallel ports, digital input/output, analog & digital, IRIG-B, and voice input/output.

    The Workstation is capable of the following functional requirements:

  • Providing a variety of mass storage devices
  • Uploading and downloading software and data to and from ground using standard ISS resources
  • Displaying high resolution graphics capable of supporting a virtual environment
  • Supporting multitasking and event-driven, real-time processing
  • Supporting multi-channel, equal-interval sampling over a range of inputs
  • Supporting Windows NT 4.0 and will provide DOS support
  • Accepting pre-developed software or COTS applications for experiment needs
  • Being compatible with investigators’ ground workstations for software/experiment development activities, including pre-flight science verification testing and baseline data collection needs
  • Being upgraded without removing the passive backplane
  • Accommodating video frame grabbing, NTSC video input and video disk storage
  • Providing voice/audio input and audio/voice output
  • Providing multiple serial interfaces
  • Providing Ethernet and Payload Bus connectivity
  • Providing an A/D Card with 16 bit resolution
  • Providing digital recording and playback capability
  • Providing support for a printer
