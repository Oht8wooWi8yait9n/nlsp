Development of Analytical Tools to Process and Apply Digitally Scanned Anthropometric Data

Record ID: Analytical_Tools
Official Source: National Aeronautics and Space Administration Life Sciences Data Archive
View Official Record on NASA NLSP →

Summary / Description

OBJECTIVES:
The proposed project type research is to enhance the core capabilities of the Habitability and Human Factors office at the Johnson Space Center (JSC) in the area of gathering and utilizing 3-dimensional whole body anthropometric data. This capability is very much in need to improve the design of space suits and other space hardware in such a way to enhance crew comfort and safety. The objectives of this project were threefold. First, develop analytical software tools to process whole-body scanned data. Second, verify, modify, and validate the analytical software results. Third, develop statistical analysis software to perform Gap analysis on existing space hardware.

APPROACH:
The deliverables included analytical software tools to extract anthropometric data (linear, curvilinear, and volumetric) from the whole-body scans. In addition, several statistical analysis codes were developed to perform Gap analyses of all existing space hardware.

RESULTS:
• Linear measurements: A study to validate linear measurements has been completed.
• Develop method to merge scan: A method to merge scans has been developed and documented.
• Develop method to fill voids: A method to fill the voids in scans has been developed and documented.
• Circumferential measurements: Circumferential measurements have been validated.
• Develop method to compute volume, mass and area of scanned segments: A software program named ‘Algor’ is being used in conjunction with the ‘ScanWorx’ and ‘Polyworks’ software to develop a method to compute the volume, area and mass properties of scanned segments. • Protocol for seated scans: A chair for the seated scans is being designed and the protocol is being developed.
• Animate segmented scans: Current Vicon model is being studied to see how it can be used as the model to drive the animation.
