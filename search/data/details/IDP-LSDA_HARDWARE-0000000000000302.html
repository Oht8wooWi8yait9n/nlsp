Thermoelectric Freezer (TEF)

Record ID: 655
Official Source: National Aeronautics and Space Administration Life Sciences Data Archive
View Official Record on NASA NLSP →

Summary / Description

The Thermoelectric Freezer (TEF) is designed to freeze biological samples (blood, urine, saliva) on the Shuttle-Mir missions. After the samples are frozen in the TEF, they are transferred to the Thermoelectric Holding Facility (TEHOF) for long term storage.

The TEF has three modes of operation: Refrigerate, Freeze and Defrost.

The TEF operates at a regulated temperature of 4 degrees Celsius +/- 2 degrees for refrigerating and at an unregulated temperature of - 20 degrees Celsius or lower for freezing. The TEF cools down to the refrigerate temperature in 10 minutes. The TEF cools down to the freezer temperature in 30 minutes. The TEF internal volume is 1050 cc and accommodates simultaneously 4 sample holder cards. The TEF will freeze 6 -12 ml test tubes containing saline solution from 25 degrees to -5 degrees Celsius in 45 minutes and is prechilled to reach a minimum of -20 degrees Celsius before the samples are added. The TEF is defrosted when visual inspection indicates it is necessary; defrosting takes 30 minutes. The TEF power supply voltage is 28 +/- 4 Volts, and consumption during initial start up and during steady state operation is less than 140 Watts. Inrush current is less than 6 amps for 0.2 seconds.

The TEF consists of two major assemblies: the active lid and the lower assembly that are separable from one another. The lower assembly is actually the cold volume of the freezer. The insulation between the cold volume an the outer case is Stephanfoam (a brand name) insulation.

Located inside the TEF cold volume is the sample holder cage, which is permanently attached. The sample holder cage is oriented such that when the sample holders are inserted, the airflow from the TEF cold side fan is parallel to the length of the sample holders. The sample holder cage consists of two aluminum plates with slots held together by four threaded stainless steel rods. The sample holder cage has four slots; the two outside slots are for urine/saliva sample holders and the two central slots are for urine/saliva sample holders and the two central slots are for blood sample holders. The sample holders cannot be inserted into the incorrect slot due to differences in the sample holders. Two latches are located on opposite sides of the lower assembly and serve to attach the lid to the lower assembly.
